# Beat-pause filler sounds

Drop short audio files here (`.wav`, `.mp3`, `.ogg`, `.flac`) — subtle human
noises like breaths, lip smacks, tsks, swallows. During every "(beat)" or
"(pause)" the app picks one at random and overlays it at low volume (-18 dB)
over the pause.

If this folder is empty, beat pauses are plain silence.
